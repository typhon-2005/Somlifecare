Best Laboratory

Tests Available

Welcome Som life care laboratory, where we offer top-quality diagnostic services at the best rates in town. Our laboratory provides a wide range of tests, including thyroid function tests (T3, T4, TSH), complete blood count (CBC), dengue NS1 antigen and antibody tests, urine routine and microscopy, blood sugar (fasting/postprandial), liver function tests (LFT), kidney function tests (KFT), lipid profile, vitamin D and B12 tests, and many more. Whether you're looking for general health screening or specific diagnostic results, we’ve got you covered. With our experienced technicians and advanced equipment, you can test yourself confidently and receive accurate, timely results at an affordable price.
